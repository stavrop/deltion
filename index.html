Deltion — Privacy Statement

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Deltion is a personal medical-records app for iPhone and iPad. It is designed to be private by default: your medical documents and the data extracted from them stay on your device. We — the developers of Deltion — do not operate any server, do not have a user account system, and cannot see your data.

What Deltion stores, and where

  • Your documents and health data (PDFs, photos, extracted measurements, summaries, reminders) are stored only on your device.
  • Files are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM, and protected with iOS file protection (NSFileProtectionComplete) so they are inaccessible while the device is locked.
  • Encryption keys and any AI provider API keys you enter are stored in the device Keychain and never leave the device.
  • There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

AI analysis (optional)

If you choose to analyse a document, the extracted text of that document is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you select and configure (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI GPT-4, or Google Gemini) using your own API key. This is the only circumstance in which document content leaves your device, and it happens only when you initiate an analysis. That data is then handled under the privacy policy of the provider you chose. Deltion does not route this through any server of ours.

Apple Health

If you enable Apple Health sync, Deltion reads and writes health samples through Apple's HealthKit on your device, with your explicit permission per data type. This data stays within Apple Health on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Bug reports and feature requests

If you use the in-app "Report a Bug or Request a Feature" form, the information you type (an optional name, the type of request, and your description) plus the app version is submitted to our public issue tracker on GitHub so we can act on it.

Because issues are public, please do not include sensitive personal or medical information in a bug report or feature request.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data. The only outbound transfers are the two you control: optional AI analysis (to your chosen provider) and feedback you choose to submit.

Children

Deltion is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this statement

We may update this statement as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions or privacy concerns can be raised on our GitHub issue tracker.

Medical disclaimer

Deltion does not provide medical advice and does not replace your doctor. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.
