Deltion does not provide medical advice and does not replace your doctor. It is a tool for keeping and reading your own records. Nothing it shows is a diagnosis, a treatment recommendation, or a substitute for professional judgement. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions, and never delay seeking care because of something the app displayed.

Two specifics worth stating plainly:

  • Summaries and extracted values are produced automatically and can be wrong. They are read out of your documents by software, including AI models. Verify anything that matters against the original report.
  • Reference ranges Deltion supplies are typical adult ranges, not your laboratory's. Where a document states its own range, that always takes precedence. Ranges the app fills in are labelled as typical, and they are context for reading a number — not a clinical threshold.

Deltion is not a diagnostic device and is not certified as a medical device. It is also not a radiology workstation: it keeps a viewable preview of an imaging series so you can carry and look at your scans, without the window/level controls, annotation or measurement tools a diagnostic viewer provides.

Three things can leave your device, all optional and all started by you: cloud AI analysis if you add your own provider key, iCloud sync if you turn it on, and a bug report if you send one. The privacy statement describes each in detail.

Because there is no copy on our side, keeping backups is your responsibility. Deleting the app, losing the device, or resetting it can lose your library. iCloud sync and your device backups are the practical safeguards.
